    How to Play

    Learning Mode (Solo)

    Play solo with zero bot wait! Land on a Ladder to answer a design check and climb (+ bonus roll). Land on a Snake to triage the outage and hold your square!

    Prevent · Detect · Recover

    Every SEV-1 Outage includes an incident debrief breaking down which control prevents it, which only detects it, and how to recover.

    SRE Shields (Guardrails)

    Land on gold guardrail squares to bank up to 2 SRE Shields. Deploy a shield on any Snake for a guaranteed instant block without risking a slide.

    1P vs Bot & Bonus Rolls

    Switch to 1P vs Bot anytime for classic arcade racing against Chaos Bot. Rolling a 6 grants an Autoscaler bonus roll—unless you slide down a Snake!
